Maggi

Home

Maggi is a quick and easy noodle dish that can be prepared in just a few minutes. It is made by cooking instant noodles in hot water along with the seasoning provided in the packet.

Maggi can also be made more flavorful by adding vegetables, eggs, or extra spices. It is a popular snack because it requires very few ingredients and little cooking time.

Ingredients

  • Maggi noodles
  • Water
  • Maggi tastemake
  • Oil or butter
  • Vegetables

Steps to make Maggi

  1. Boil water in a pan
  2. Add Maggi noodles and tastemaker
  3. Add vegetables if you want
  4. Cook for 2–3 minutes
  5. Mix well and serve hot
