Omelette

Home

An omelet is a simple and quick dish made with beaten eggs, salt, and spices. It is cooked in a pan with a little oil or butter until the eggs are firm.

You can also add ingredients like onions, tomatoes, chilies, or cheese to make it more flavorful. An omelet is usually served hot with bread or toast.

Ingredients

  • Eggs – 2
  • Onion – 1 small
  • Green chilli – 1
  • Salt – to taste
  • Black pepper – ¼ teaspoon
  • Oil or butter – 1 teaspoon

Steps to make omelet

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them.
  2. Add salt, pepper, onion, and green chilli.
  3. Heat oil in a pan.
  4. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  5. Cook both sides until golden
  6. Serve hot.
