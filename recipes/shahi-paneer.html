Shahi Paneer Recipe

Home

Shahi Paneer is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with soft pieces of paneer cooked in a smooth, flavorful gravy. The gravy is usually prepared with onions, tomatoes, cashews, cream, and aromatic spices such as cardamom, cumin, garam masala, and turmeric. The combination of creamy texture and mild spices gives Shahi Paneer its distinctive royal taste.

To prepare Shahi Paneer, the onion, tomato, and cashew mixture is cooked and blended into a smooth paste. The paste is then cooked with spices and cream before adding the paneer cubes. After simmering for a few minutes, the paneer absorbs the flavors of the gravy and becomes soft and delicious. Shahi Paneer is commonly served hot with naan, roti, or rice.

Ingredients

  • Paneer – 250 g
  • Onion – 1 medium
  • Tomatoes – 2 medium
  • Cashews – 10–15
  • Fresh cream – 3–4 tablespoons
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 1 teaspoon
  • Butter or oil – 2 tablespoons
  • Milk – ½ cup
  • Garam masala – ½ teaspoon
  • Turmeric powder – ¼ teaspoon
  • Red chilli powder – ½ teaspoon
  • Coriander powder – 1 teaspoon
  • Cumin seeds – ½ teaspoon
  • Cardamom – 2
  • Cinnamon – 1 small piece
  • Salt – to taste
  • Water – as needed
  • Fresh coriander – for garnishing

Steps to Make Shahi Paneer

  1. Prepare the paneer: Cut 250 g paneer into small cubes. You can lightly fry the cubes in butter or oil until golden.
  2. Prepare the gravy: Heat oil or butter in a pan. Add chopped onions, tomatoes, cashews, ginger, and garlic. Cook until soft.
  3. Blend the mixture: Let the cooked mixture cool slightly, then blend it into a smooth paste.
  4. Cook the spices: Heat some butter in the pan and add cumin seeds, turmeric, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Cook for a few seconds.
  5. Add the gravy: Add the blended paste to the pan and cook for 5–7 minutes. Add some water if the gravy is too thick.
  6. Add cream and milk: Add milk and fresh cream, then mix well. Cook on low heat for a few minutes.
  7. Add paneer: Put the paneer cubes into the gravy and simmer for 3–5 minutes so the paneer absorbs the flavors.
  8. Garnish and serve: Add fresh coriander or a little cream on top. Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.
